# Evidence and claim boundaries

## Supported by the recovered system

- A local Python and SQLite application with a browser interface
- A prospect pipeline containing more than 4,000 records
- Deterministic multi-factor scoring and priority bands
- Website-status checks
- Local-model email generation with validation and template fallback
- Individual and bounded-batch Gmail draft creation
- Draft-only delivery logic

## Deliberately generalized

- Exact customer and message counts
- Scoring weights and internal thresholds
- Risk dictionaries and proprietary business rules
- Private workflow terminology
- Development dates and internal milestones

## Not claimed

- Revenue or conversion impact
- Fully autonomous outreach
- Sole manual authorship of every source-code line
- Public deployment of the private system
- Permission to distribute the original implementation or data
